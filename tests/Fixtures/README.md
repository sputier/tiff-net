# Test fixtures

Where each fixture comes from, so that it can be regenerated and its licence is known.

| Folder | Content | Origin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `interop/manifest.json` | the list of the tifffile corpus and the SHA-256 of the samples of each dataset | written by `tools/make_fixtures.py --update`; committed |
| `interop/generated/` | the corpus itself (about 6 000 files, 140 MB): 11 sample types × 1 to 4 channels (extra channel unspecified, associated or unassociated alpha) × compressions × predictors × strips/tiles × interleaved/planar × classic/BigTIFF × little/big endian, 53 × 37 pixels. Predictor 2 on 64-bit integers, which tifffile does not write, is encoded by the script itself and checked by reading it back with tifffile | written by `python -I tools/make_fixtures.py` with the versions of `tools/requirements-interop.txt`; not committed, generated by the `interop` CI job |

All the data of the corpus is synthetic, drawn from a seeded generator: there is no third-party image and no licence question.
