# Measurements of 2026-11-06

Reference machine: the 8-core laptop of the review (x64, AVX2, .NET 10.0, Windows 11), warm cache, best of 5 to 15 runs, other
work running (variations of about 10 %). Images of 24 MP (6 000 x 4 000), smooth with noise as astronomical frames are: u16
grey, f32 RGB. Reproduced by `TargetBenchmarks` (BenchmarkDotNet) and, for JPEG, by `tools/bench_jpeg.py` and `JpegBenchmarks`.

## The targets of the architecture

| Scenario | First implementation | Target | Measured | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Read u16 24 MP, none | 4.4 GB/s | ≥ 4 GB/s | 4.3 GB/s (1 thread), 4.0 GB/s (8) | met |
| Read u16 24 MP, Deflate + horizontal predictor | 160 MB/s | ≥ 1 GB/s on 8 cores, ≥ 250 MB/s on 1 | 1.40 GB/s on 8, 300 MB/s on 1 | met |
| Read f32 RGB 24 MP, Deflate + floating point predictor | 143 MB/s, 654 MB allocated | ≥ 1 GB/s on 8 cores; allocations ≤ 1.05 × the pixels | 2.1 GB/s on 8 (441 MB/s on 1); 1.01 × the pixels | met |
| Write u16 24 MP, Deflate + predictor | 4.4 s | ≤ 0.5 s on 8 cores | 0.13 to 0.19 s (0.6 s on 1) | met |
| Write f32 RGB 24 MP, Deflate + predictor | 8.0 s | ≤ 1 s on 8 cores | 0.86 s (4.6 s on 1) | met |
| Peak memory, read f32 compressed | 2.05 × the pixels | ≤ 1.1 × the pixels | ≤ 1.1 × (MemoryPeakTests, 144 MB on 4 threads) | met |
| Read 24 MP baseline JPEG RGB | (not measured) | within 2 × of libjpeg-turbo through Pillow on 1 core | 1.1 × (grey), 1.2 × (4:4:4), 1.35 × (4:2:0), 1.3 × (progressive) | met |

## JPEG against libjpeg-turbo (12 MP, quality 90, one thread)

| Image | libjpeg-turbo (Pillow) | Eleris.TiffNet | Ratio |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| grey | 34 ms | 38 ms | 1.1 |
| YCbCr 4:4:4 | 82 ms | 98 ms | 1.2 |
| YCbCr 4:2:0 | 48 ms | 65 ms | 1.35 |
| progressive 4:2:0 | 145 ms | 190 ms | 1.3 |

What brought them there: an AVX2 islow IDCT (bit-exact with the scalar one; the scalar one stays where AVX2 is missing, ARM
included), an 11-bit Huffman lookup with whole coefficients, an 8-byte bit refill, colour converted row by row.
